# Product Guidelines: Sonar Vault

## Design Philosophy
- **Modern & Clean UI:** The interface should feature a spacious layout with clear typography, focusing on ease of use and navigability without overwhelming the user with unnecessary visual noise.

## Tone and Prose
- **Formal & Technical Messaging:** All system communications, alerts, and documentation should be direct, precise, and utilize correct naval and engineering terminology. Avoid casual or conversational language.

## Error Handling UX
- **Detailed Diagnostics:** When errors occur, the system must prioritize transparency over simplicity. Error screens should surface full stack traces, forensic codes, and detailed diagnostic information to assist the Testing and BSTC departments in rapid troubleshooting.

## Accessibility
- **High Contrast:** Given the variable lighting conditions inherent to shipboard environments, the application must maintain high visual contrast to ensure readability and operational safety at all times.
